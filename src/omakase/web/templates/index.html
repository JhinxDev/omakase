Know your counter.

Bring your own key. Keep your history yours.

What do I need to try it?

A model key from one of the listed providers, plus a public AniList username or a MyAnimeList XML export. Taste notes are optional. Provider charges and model availability depend on your own account.

How does live model selection work?

When you paste a provider API key and click Load models, Omakase asks that provider's official /models endpoint for the models enabled on your account, tags them by speed or reasoning, and scrubs the key immediately after the response. You can also enter any model ID manually at any time.

What happens to my API key and watch history?

Your key, watch history, and notes stay in request-local memory to prepare one recommendation menu with your chosen provider. Nothing is saved to disk, logs, cookies, or a database, and the entered key is cleared as soon as the menu arrives.

What is Omakase Plus, and can I self-host?

Omakase is open-source (CLI, web UI, and stdio MCP server) and self-hosting supports local model runtimes on your own machine. Omakase Plus is a separate private media-library companion not connected to this public counter.
